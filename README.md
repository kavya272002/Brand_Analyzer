# 🚀 Brand & Industry AI Analyzer

A professional-grade, AI-first strategic analysis tool designed for MBA students, consultants, and business analysts. Powered by **Google Gemini 1.5 Flash**, it generates comprehensive strategic frameworks for **any** brand or industry globally.

![Brand Analyzer Screenshot](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1460925895917-afdab827c52f?auto=format&fit=crop&q=80&w=1200)

## ✨ Features

- **Any Brand, Any Industry**: Not restricted to a database. Analyze "Ather Energy", "The Indian EV Market", or "Global SaaS Trends" instantly.
- **Comprehensive Frameworks**:
  - **PESTEL Analysis**: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, and Legal factors.
  - **SWOT Matrix**: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats.
  - **Porter's Five Forces**: Competitive rivalry, supplier power, buyer power, etc.
  - **Industry Gaps**: Identifies market weaknesses.
  - **Strategic Recommendations**: Actionable, high-level business advice.
  - **Ansoff Matrix**: Strategic growth options.
  - **Value Chain Analysis**: End-to-end operational insights.
- **BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)**: Use your free Google Gemini API key for zero-cost, private analysis.
- **Modern Dashboard**: Sleek, glassmorphic UI with PDF export and side-by-side comparison mode.

## 🛠️ How to Use

1. **Get a Free API Key**: Visit [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey) (Free, no credit card needed).
2. **Setup**: Click the ⚙️ icon in the app and paste your key.
3. **Analyze**: Enter any brand or industry in the search bar.
4. **Share**: Use the "Share" button to send your analysis to others!

## 🔧 Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Vanilla HTML5, CSS3 (Custom Design System), JavaScript (ES6+).
- **AI Engine**: Google Gemini 1.5 Flash (via Generative Language API).
- **Deployment**: GitHub Pages.
